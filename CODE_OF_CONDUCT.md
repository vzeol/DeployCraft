# Community conduct

DeployCraft welcomes people with different backgrounds, experience levels and ways of contributing. These rules
apply to the project's issues, pull requests, reviews and other spaces moderated by the maintainer. They apply
to everyone participating, including the maintainer.

## Working together

- Discuss the problem and the proposed change. Explain disagreements without attacking the person.
- Be patient with people learning GitHub, administration or programming. Asking for clarification is welcome.
- Give feedback that helps someone take the next step. Respect a contributor's time and a maintainer's decision
  about the scope of the project.
- Keep contributions relevant and give credit for other people's work.
- Protect people's privacy. Do not publish credentials, private messages or identifying information about a
  person or their environment without permission.

Harassment, discriminatory remarks, threats, sexual comments or imagery, repeated personal attacks and
deliberate disruption are not acceptable. Do not pressure someone to deploy untested changes to a real fleet
or to disclose private information.

## Raising a concern

To raise a concern, comment briefly on the public discussion or open an issue, linking to it rather than
copying abusive content. Do not post personal information or private evidence. If you already have a private way
to reach the maintainer, you may use it instead; the project does not provide a dedicated private conduct inbox.

For content that violates GitHub's policies, use GitHub's reporting tools. If the concern involves the maintainer
and you do not want to raise it with them, use those tools. Do not use the security vulnerability reporting
channel for conduct complaints; that channel is reserved for software vulnerabilities.

## Moderation

The maintainer may ask for a change of tone, edit or remove content, lock a discussion, or restrict participation
when necessary. The response should reflect the severity and repetition of the behavior. A serious threat or
privacy violation can require immediate action.

When practical, the maintainer explains the moderation decision without exposing private information. A person
may ask for reconsideration respectfully. Retaliation against someone who raises a concern is not acceptable.
